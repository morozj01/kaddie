Kaddie {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in namespace kaddie-system.

The system is Halted. The chart never creates SREConfig/default, and a missing
SREConfig means Halted for every component. When you are ready to enable it:

  kubectl apply -f - <<'YAML'
  apiVersion: kaddie.org/v1alpha1
  kind: SREConfig
  metadata:
    name: default
  spec:
    mode: Enabled
    reason: "Enabled after review"
  YAML

Helm doesn't own SREConfig/default: upgrades, uninstalls and reinstalls leave
it, and its settings, untouched.
